Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026 — Your privacy is fundamental to how we operate.

01

Data Collection

We collect only the necessary information to manage your space on the network: your name, email, uploaded image, and the link you provide. We do not collect any additional personal data beyond what is required to deliver our service.

02

Cookies & IP Tracking

We use cookies and track IP addresses solely to ensure the integrity of the "Top 25 Clicks" system and to prevent manipulation through automated bots. This data is used exclusively for anti-fraud purposes and is never sold or shared.

03

Payment Security

We do not view, access, or store your credit card or payment information. All transactions are processed through Stripe and PayPal using international SSL 256-bit encryption. Your financial data never touches our servers.

04

Third Parties

Your personal data is never sold, rented, or shared with any external parties for marketing purposes. We may use trusted service providers (such as payment processors) solely to deliver our service, and they are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
