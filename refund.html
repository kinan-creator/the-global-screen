Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026 — We are committed to full transparency with our customers.

01

Refund Before Publishing

You are entitled to request a full refund within 10 minutes of payment, provided your image has not yet appeared on the global network. Due to the nature of manual processing, this window must be respected strictly.

02

Content Standards & Rejection

We maintain a professional and safe environment for all. If uploaded content (image or link) violates our ethical or legal standards, the request will be immediately rejected and the full amount returned to your account automatically with no additional fees.

03

Policy After Publishing

Once your space is live on the network, the service is considered fully delivered. Therefore, refunds are not possible after publishing.

Note for Investors: Your digital space is an asset you own. If you decide to exit, you can wait until all spaces are sold to participate in the Resale Marketplace, where you can sell your position to other investors and potentially profit — similar to the experience of limited digital assets.

04

Refund Processing Time

Upon approval of a refund, the amount is sent immediately. It typically takes 5–10 business days to appear in your bank account, depending on your bank's policy.
